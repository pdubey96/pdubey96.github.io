Publications & Preprints

My work centers on two intertwined lines: optimal multiple testing and federated, communication-constrained learning, together with their growing intersection. Author order is by contribution, and all papers are joint with my advisor, Xiaoming Huo, except where noted. See Google Scholar or my CV for the latest.

  2. Most Powerful Tests with Exact Family-Wise Error Rate Control: Necessary Conditions and Fast Computation for General K

    Prasanjit Dubey and Xiaoming Huo

    Revision under review Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics (JCGS) · 2025 Paper

    Companion paper Necessary Conditions and a Path to Fast Computing, arXiv · 2025

    An efficient algorithm for the most powerful test under exact FWER control for an arbitrary number of exchangeable hypotheses. The power advantage over Bonferroni, Holm, and Hommel grows with the number of hypotheses (an 84% relative gain at K = 12 in the truncated-normal model).

    ★ Invited talk, Optimisation for Statistics and Learning session, INFORMS Annual Meeting, 2025
